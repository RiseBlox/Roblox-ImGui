Its `wally.toml` declares MIT and its README states that the port is distributed under the same MIT license as Dear ImGui. The imported code's original comments and file-level notices are retained.

The port originated from [GrayWolf64/imgui-lua](https://github.com/GrayWolf64/imgui-lua), as documented by the 4DBug project.
